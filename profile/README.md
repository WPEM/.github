# AI XRD Analyzer: **[WPEM](https://bin-cao.github.io/PyWPEM)**

---

## About WPEM

**WPEM (Whole Pattern Evolution Model)** is a research project dedicated to resolving complex crystal structures and heavily overlapping Bragg peaks in **mixed X-ray diffraction and polycrystalline powder diffraction data**.

The project is led by **[Bin Cao](https://bin-cao.github.io/)** and focuses on combining **physics-based diffraction theory with modern AI techniques** to improve the reliability and precision of structural characterization.

---

## Research Capabilities

The WPEM framework enables several advanced diffraction analysis tasks:

- **Detection of subtle structural differences**  
  (e.g., distinguishing **α phase vs. α′ phase in Ti–Ni alloys**)

- **Separation of amorphous and crystalline components**  
  in complex polymer systems such as **polybutene**

- **Analysis of complex solid-solution structures**  
  in multi-component materials

- **Computation and interpretation of diffraction scattering information**  
  across the entire diffraction pattern

---

## Project Vision

WPEM aims to build a **high-precision computational framework for X-ray diffraction analysis**, enabling more reliable structural identification in complex materials systems.

The project started in **2020** with a long-term goal of promoting **open and reproducible scientific research in diffraction analysis**.
